What we're actually doing here

Hosting the agent infra on our own cloud: one pod in the CoreDev EKS cluster running Metabase + an OpenCode agent, reaching a dev warehouse over the VPC. Right now we're proving the plumbing with a throwaway spike pod.

Cluster: coredev-coredev-1 (us-east-1) Account: CoreDev 488208231899 Branch: robo-de-eks-alpha Mode: alpha — OK if it breaks

AThe flow we're building (target end-state)

A user asks the robot for analytics work; the control plane routes it to the in-cluster pod; the agent drives Metabase via the mb CLI; Metabase queries the warehouse natively over the VPC; results stream back. This is what "running the agent infra on our cloud" means, concretely.

BWhat we're doing right now — the spike

Before any of that, we prove the two boring-but-critical facts: can we schedule a pod in this cluster, and can a pod reach a warehouse over the VPC. That's the throwaway metabase-spike-pod.yaml — plain Metabase + a netshoot shell, no agent yet.

The one-line answer: we're moving the robot off Modal and onto our own EKS cluster as a single pod, because for one tenant that's simpler and the pod can reach our warehouses natively. Right now we're at the very first proof — "can a pod here even talk to a warehouse." Everything else hangs off that yes.
